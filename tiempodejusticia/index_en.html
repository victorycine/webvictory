SYNOPSIS

Years after the murder of his wife, Víctor, a former police officer consumed by guilt and a desire for justice, receives a new lead about the person responsible for the crime.

Determined to put an end to the search that has defined his life, he infiltrates a dangerous criminal organization where he discovers Camila, a young woman who has been kidnapped by the gang.

From that moment on, what began as a mission of revenge becomes a desperate race to rescue her, as he confronts a web of violence, corruption, and secrets that will test everything he once believed in.

THE PROJECT

Time for Justice is an independent action feature film with a strong dramatic component, produced by Victory Cine. The story takes place in the city of Rosario, which becomes the main setting for an uncommon proposal within Argentine cinema: an action thriller that combines high-impact sequences with characters shaped by pain, loss, and the search for justice.

With more than 90% of the film already shot, the project is moving toward its final stage of production. The plan includes participation in international film festivals, a promotional strategy through paid advertising campaigns on social media, and a theatrical release.

Previous productions by Victory Cine have been selected at festivals in the United States, Spain, the United Kingdom, and India, and have also had in-person screenings as part of the festival circuit in Argentina.

At this stage, the project is seeking partnerships with producers, companies, and brands that wish to associate themselves with a film with national and international reach potential.

In addition to their participation as sponsors, partners will receive communication initiatives and content specifically developed to enhance their brand visibility during the launch of the project.
