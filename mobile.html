About

VICTORY Cine was founded in 2005 after several years of experience in cinematic stunt work.

What began as a passion for action in front of the camera naturally evolved into a new stage: telling our own stories.

Today we develop independent films with a strong visual identity and a focus on movement, emotion and cinematic storytelling.
